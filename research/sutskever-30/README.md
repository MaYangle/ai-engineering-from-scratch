## How to study a source

1. Follow the core lesson first; use its prerequisites and exercises as the learning scaffold.
2. Open the linked card and go to the cited primary source. The 30 items include papers, tutorials, books, an essay, and a university course.
3. Compare the source's method and evidence with the paired notebook. Each card states what the notebook does and does not demonstrate.
4. Attempt the two retrieval questions before revealing the explanations; use the paper tutor for a one-question-at-a-time discussion.
