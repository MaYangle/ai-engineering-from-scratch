### 1. What changes when the dilation rate increases?

<details>
<summary>Reveal explanation</summary>

Kernel sample points spread farther apart, expanding the effective receptive field without adding kernel parameters.

</details>

### 2. Why preserve feature-map resolution for dense prediction?

<details>
<summary>Reveal explanation</summary>

Predictions are needed at many spatial positions; dilation adds context without downsampling the map.

</details>
