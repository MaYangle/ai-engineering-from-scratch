## Retrieval practice

Try each question from memory before revealing the explanation.

### 1. What are DPR's two encoders and retrieval score?

<details>
<summary>Reveal explanation</summary>

Separate question and passage encoders produce dense vectors; rank passages by inner product with the question vector.

</details>

### 2. Why use in-batch passages as negatives?

<details>
<summary>Reveal explanation</summary>

They train the question vector to prefer its paired passage over other batch passages; false negatives can occur.

</details>
