### 1. Which state does the forget gate act on?

<details>
<summary>Reveal explanation</summary>

The cell state; it controls how much of the previous cell information is retained.

</details>

### 2. How do cell state and hidden state differ?

<details>
<summary>Reveal explanation</summary>

The cell state carries gated memory; the hidden state is the exposed output used by the next step and downstream layers.

</details>
