Try each question from memory before revealing the explanation.

### 1. Why scale query–key dot products by √dₖ?

<details>
<summary>Reveal explanation</summary>

Large dimensions can create large logits and an overly sharp softmax; scaling stabilizes range and gradients.

</details>

### 2. Why add positional information to self-attention?

<details>
<summary>Reveal explanation</summary>

Attention alone does not encode sequence order, so positional information distinguishes token positions.

</details>
