### 1. What pattern did the paper often observe as evidence moved through a long context?

<details>
<summary>Reveal explanation</summary>

### 2. What should remain fixed when testing evidence-position effects?

<details>
<summary>Reveal explanation</summary>

Model, prompt, evidence, context length, distractors, and evaluation; change only the evidence position.
