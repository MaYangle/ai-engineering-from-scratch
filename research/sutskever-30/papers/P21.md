## Retrieval practice

Try each question from memory before revealing the explanation.

### 1. Why does CTC include a blank symbol?

<details>
<summary>Reveal explanation</summary>

It lets variable-length frame paths map to a target string without frame-level alignment.

</details>

### 2. Why does [h,h,e] collapse to he while [h,blank,h] becomes hh?

<details>
<summary>Reveal explanation</summary>

CTC removes blanks and merges adjacent repeats; the blank separates the two h symbols.

</details>
